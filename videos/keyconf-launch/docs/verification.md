## Encoded artifact

Final export validation is recorded in `frame-export.json` and `video-probe.json`. These files are regenerated by `npm run verify:render` against the actual MP4. The render script checks the complete frame sequence and encodes from lossless PNGs before that verification.

The delivered MP4 is 14,988,012 bytes and decodes to exactly 1,080 frames at 1920×1080 and 30 fps. Video and audio are both 36.000 seconds. Its stereo 48 kHz AAC track measures -19.08 LUFS integrated and -1.19 dBTP. Full decoding succeeds with zero near-black intervals. Rec.709 primaries, transfer and matrix tags are explicit. The actual encoded contact sheet and full-resolution scene frames were inspected.

The opening headline was delayed after motion-frame review. Its 69 affected frames were recaptured through Hyperframes at exact frame times; decoded pixels at both splice boundaries matched the original lossless sequence exactly. The final full composition check passes with the retimed source. The updated complete-render script reproduces that timing without a repair step.
