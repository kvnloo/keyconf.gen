# Premium / custom / retro-display keyboard inventory

Research pass checked 2026-09-08. This is an initial source-backed set of **80 distinct model identities**. A revision is a separate row only when the maker names a materially different product or generation; colorways, keycap bundles, and ordinary finish choices are collapsed into the parent model. Prices are recorded only when the official page exposed a price; they are not estimates and should not be interpreted as today’s secondary-market value.

`Full` means a complete keyboard or assembled product. `Kit` means case/PCB/plate or an aftermarket housing that needs an OEM donor. `Accessory` is included only where it is a display or keyboard-adjacent product worth tracking separately. “CAD status” is evidence status, not a claim that a file exists: `explicit` means the maker exposes CAD/plate/3D files, `TBA` means the maker mentions a future release, and `not surfaced` means the checked official page did not expose a file link.

## Scope and gaps

The table currently has **80 rows**, with **80 distinct model identities after collapsing ordinary colorways**. The initial target was 40+; this pass exceeds it, but it is still a curated first tranche rather than a claim that hundreds have been researched.

The strongest current-price evidence is for Angry Miao, Qwertykeys, HHKB, Wuque, NovelKeys, Norbauer, RAMA legacy pages, Werk One, and the Mode store/archive. Mode, Geon, and Singa expose unusually good long-term documentation, but many individual historical boards do not retain a primary price page. For those rows the price is deliberately blank or marked unverified.

The biggest inventory gaps for a next pass are Keycult (official specification/resource pages were found, but model sale/price pages need a focused audit), TGR models outside the Singa collaboration, Matrix Lab’s current product pages, Owlab models, NovelKeys’ current Classic-TKL variants, display-first boards beyond Angry Miao/MelGeek, and accessory/display docks. The Keycult resource set is especially useful for CAD/plate evidence, but it should not be used to invent a current sale price.

## Source notes

- [Mode Keyboard Compendium](https://compendium.modedesigns.com/) is the maker-maintained index. It explicitly separates `Current` vs `Legacy`, gives production end years, and states that every keyboard has design files. The individual archive pages provide the clearest feature descriptions for Envoy, 2024 SixtyFive, and 2022 Sonnet.
- [Geon resources](https://geon.works/pages/resources), [plate files](https://geon.works/pages/plate-files), and [compatibility pages](https://geon.works/pages/plate-and-case-compatibility) expose the maker’s 3D render, DWG/STEP, plate, PCB, and compatibility evidence. The F1-8X page gives the 2,900-unit historical sales figure; the W1-AT page gives the 650-unit figure.
- [Singa downloads](https://singakbd.com/pages/downloads) is the primary model list used for Singa rows. It names firmware/plate resources for Unikorn generations, Kohaku/Kyohaku, Jaguar, Ocelot, Rubrehaku, and Singa R1. No price is inferred from those downloads.
- [Angry Miao’s product pages](https://www.angrymiao.com/en/products/) and [store collections](https://store.angrymiao.com/collections/cyberboard) explicitly describe LED panels/matrices, RGB/lighting, gasket or leaf-spring mounts, finishes, and the displayed current/list prices.
- [RAMA legacy keyboards](https://ramaworks.store/collections/legacy-keyboards) is used for historical price ranges; [RAMA technical pages](https://rama.works/m60-a) and [RAMA resources](https://ramaworks.store/pages/resources) are used for material and CAD/plate evidence.
- [Norbauer](https://www.norbauer.co/collections/housings) is aftermarket housing inventory. Heavy-6 compatibility is explicitly FC660C-only; Heavy Grail is explicitly for HHKB; these are kits, not complete keyboards.

- [NovelKeys](https://novelkeys.com/collections/keyboards/nonreturnable) pages clearly label archived/retired products and expose original/clearance prices. The NK65 product pages provide case, plate, PCB, and layout details.
- [PFU/Ricoh’s US store](https://store.pfu-us.ricoh.com/keyboards/) is used for HHKB current list/sale prices and product families. The 30th-anniversary Japanese release is intentionally not split into colorway rows.
