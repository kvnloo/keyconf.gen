# Visual direction

The keyboard is the main subject. A continuous Three.js camera shows its form, changes of color, separating layers and physical key travel. Real enlarged interface crops provide evidence of the app’s controls. Each shot has one main action, followed by enough stillness to read or listen.

## Color and materials

Dark forest ground (#111713), ivory type (#E9EDE6), sage accent (#C4D1B9). Forest Line uses ivory alphas, forest modifiers and a sage spacebar. Blush and Midnight appear as alternate colorways. Captured UI keeps its actual colors.

The 3D study uses sculpted plastic keycaps, a metal case, translucent switch housings, a perforated metal plate and green PCB. Reflection lighting, contact shadows, fine bump texture and restrained macro depth of field describe these surfaces. The model is representative, not vendor CAD. Avoid overlays that pretend to be material detail.

## Type and composition

Local Liberation Sans is registered as KeyconfFilmSans; local JetBrains Mono as KeyconfFilmMono. No optical-size axis is assumed. Display type is usually 96–140px, short and sentence case. Captions use roughly 28–30px. Keep important type 100px from horizontal edges and 72px from the top.

Use the clear left or upper field for short copy. Keep the keyboard large, with intentional cropping only in macro shots. The real typing widget sits above the complete board. The component card keeps legible product names and specs. The final URL holds through the end. No mandatory scene numbers, decorative counters, monogram stamp or repeated screenshot card template.

## Motion

Camera poses, targets, geometry and materials derive from absolute film time. Smooth, directed acceleration connects shots. Color waves and staggered layer reveals explain construction and customization. Text enters after the relevant product movement and settles for reading. Sound events motivate key travel. The music recedes during the recorded audition.
