Reviewed September 6, 2026 against [Keychron's keyboard page](https://www.keychron.com/products/keychron-q1-he-8k-magnetic-switch-keyboard) and [Lime switch page](https://www.keychron.com/products/keychron-ultra-fast-lime-magnetic-switch).

Added a separate 75% factory assembly with six component references. It uses Lime, an aluminum case and plate, PCB screw-in stabilizers and the supplied OSA PBT keycap reference. These are assembly components, not claims of separate retail availability. The scene remains an illustrative 75% model.

The keyboard page's opening compatibility note lists Lime and Gateron Jade, but its specifications still say Lime only. Jade therefore remains unknown here. Original Q1 HE Double-Rail references do not establish fit in this model. Mechanical contact switches conflict with its magnetic PCB. The wired polling choices do not establish end-to-end latency.

Tests distinguish the assemblies, verify the Lime pairing, preserve unknown magnetic pairings and reject mechanical contact switches. Mixed original-Q1-HE and 8K core parts do not inherit documented assembly fit. The existing all-assembly tests cover portable encoding and undo.
